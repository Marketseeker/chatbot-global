Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Chatbot Global ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website or talk to our chatbot, how we use it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and short.

Who we are

Chatbot Global provides chatbots for service companies. Our business is operated by Chatbot Global, Texas, United States. You can reach us at hello@chatbotglobal.com.

Information we collect

  • What you tell the chatbot. Messages you type to our assistant, and any details you choose to share, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, the kind of service you offer and what you are looking for.
  • Usage data. Through Google Analytics we receive information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This data is collected using cookies and similar technologies.
  • Setup details for customers. If you get a chatbot from us, we keep the contact and website details needed to send you your code and help you install it.

Please do not share sensitive information in the chat, such as payment card numbers, government ID numbers or health information. We do not need it.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and provide the chatbot.
  • To follow up with you about Chatbot Global, if you asked us to or shared your details with us.
  • To send customers their chatbot code and help them install it, and to deliver leads.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Chatbots on a company's own website

When a visitor talks to a chatbot we have set up on a service company's website, that company is the one who receives the conversation and the lead details, such as the visitor's name, contact information and request. We process that information on the company's behalf. The company's own privacy practices apply to how it uses the lead.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and the chatbot, and only as needed:

  • Chat platform provider. Our chat widget is provided by a third-party platform that processes the messages you send.
  • Google Analytics, for website usage statistics.
  • Email and hosting providers, which deliver leads and host the site.
  • The service company you contacted, when you submit your details through its chatbot.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or in connection with a sale or reorganization of our business.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on. The chat widget may also use cookies or local storage to keep your conversation going.

How long we keep it

We keep chat conversations and lead details only as long as we need them for the purposes above, or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email hello@chatbotglobal.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional legal rights, and we will honor them.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our site and chatbot are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

International visitors

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws than where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@chatbotglobal.com.
